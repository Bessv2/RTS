Turn on multi-factor authentication today

Stolen passwords are behind a huge share of account break-ins. Multi-factor authentication is the simplest, cheapest fix.

Passwords get reused, guessed and leaked. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) adds a second check, like a code on your phone, so a stolen password alone isn't enough.

Where to turn it on first

  • Your email account (it can reset everything else)
  • Banking and payroll
  • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace admin accounts
  • Your domain registrar and website host

Which method to choose

  1. An authenticator app or security key is the strongest everyday option.
  2. Text-message codes are better than nothing, but easier to intercept.

It takes about ten minutes per account, and it's one of the best security investments a small business can make.
